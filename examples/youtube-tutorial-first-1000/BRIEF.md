## Intent

A faceless screen tutorial in the shape of the popular "make money with web design" YouTube videos: a hook that names the wrong way, one clear system, then the client work walked through on the real product. Confident, plain, no hype. The product on screen is the product: real Hanzo captures, never drawn mock-ups.

## Customizations

- Burned-in captions from word timings, one caption skin for the whole piece
- Voiceover on Kokoro `am_adam` (offline; no HeyGen sign-in on this machine)
- A subtle music bed under the voice, carved
- Typed prompts and cursor moves are animated over the real screens (prompt-type-submit-generate, cursor-ui-demo); the screens themselves are captures
